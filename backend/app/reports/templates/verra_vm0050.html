5. Emission Reductions

5.1 Calculation Method

Emission reductions were calculated as the difference between baseline and project emissions, minus leakage, in accordance with VM0050 Section 8:

ER = BE - PE - LK

Where ER = emission reductions, BE = baseline emissions, PE = project emissions, LK = leakage.
